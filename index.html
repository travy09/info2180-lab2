Exploring Digital Art and 2D Animation

September 28, 2026 by Trahvall Mckenzie

When I am not working in an Ubuntu terminal, I enjoy diving into digital art and 2D animation. Lately, I have been spending my free time sketching on my Ugee UE16 display tablet and experimenting with different brush configurations in Krita and OpenToonz.

Working on original pieces, like my recent digital artwork titled "The Cave," provides a perfect creative balance to computer science. Whether I am animating frames in Callipeg or finalizing canvas settings, bringing an illustration to life is always incredibly rewarding.

Perfecting the Weekend Recipe

September 28, 2026 by Trahvall Mckenzie

Weekends are usually my chance to take a break from coding and spend some time in the kitchen. One of my favourite things to make is Jamaican jerk chicken. I have been experimenting with different seasonings and cooking methods to get the right balance of spice, flavour, and tenderness.

Cooking has taught me that getting a recipe right takes patience and practice. Even when I follow the same steps, there is always something small I can improve the next time. Once I finally perfect my jerk chicken, my next weekend challenge will be making homemade bread pudding.
